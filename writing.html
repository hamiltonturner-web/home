Writing & Editing

Words that need to be exact

Foreign policy and funder-facing writing gets read by people ready to push back on every claim. The writing and editing here is built to hold up: precise, sourced, legible.

ANALYSIS

Policy & foreign affairs writing

Western Sahara rarely makes headlines — the policy decisions shaping it still do. Longform analysis on U.S. foreign policy and the Middle East and North Africa, covering conflict, self-determination movements, and rights issues, written for a general but informed audience.

FACT-CHECKING

Research & sourcing

A single unverified claim can undercut an entire piece of reporting. Fact-checking and sourcing support for political journalism, including coverage of civil society and rights issues in North Africa.

EDITING

Editing & proofreading

A clean sentence can still carry a weak argument. Line editing and proofreading for essays, reports, and funder narratives, with as much attention to the argument as the sentence.

GHOSTWRITING

Drafting & ghostwriting

Knowing what you want to say and getting it onto the page clearly are two different skills. First drafts and structural rewrites for people who have the first but need help with the second.
