Grants & Operations

Where I help nonprofits

Most of this work happens inside organizations that are small by design but international by necessity — a handful of staff spread across time zones, currencies, and legal jurisdictions, answering to funders who each want something slightly different.

LIFECYCLE

Grants lifecycle management

Prospecting, proposal development, budget construction, compliance tracking, and narrative and financial reporting, run as one continuous system instead of separate fire drills.

SYSTEMS

CRM build-out

Salesforce and Airtable environments built to track relationships, deadlines, and disbursements, so grant status lives in one place instead of someone's memory.

MEASUREMENT

MEL frameworks

Monitoring, evaluation, and learning structures that produce reportable evidence without turning program staff into full-time data entry.

STRUCTURE

Cross-border compliance

Budgeting and reporting across currencies and legal structures, including guidance for organizations navigating Dutch stichting registration and funder eligibility across the Netherlands, Morocco, and the US.
