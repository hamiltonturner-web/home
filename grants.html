Grants & Operations

Where I help nonprofits

Most of this work happens inside organizations that are small by design but international by necessity: a handful of staff spread across time zones, currencies, and legal jurisdictions, answering to funders who each want something slightly different.

LIFECYCLE

Grants lifecycle management

I run prospecting, proposal development, budget construction, compliance tracking, and narrative and financial reporting as one continuous system, not separate fire drills.

SYSTEMS

CRM build-out

I build Salesforce and Airtable environments that track relationships, deadlines, and disbursements, so grant status lives in one place instead of someone's memory.

MEASUREMENT

MEL frameworks

I design monitoring, evaluation, and learning structures that produce reportable evidence without turning program staff into full-time data entry.

STRUCTURE

Cross-border compliance

I budget and report across currencies and legal structures, and guide organizations through Dutch stichting registration and funder eligibility across the Netherlands, Morocco, and the U.S.
